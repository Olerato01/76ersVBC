76ERS VC is a community-based club that develops volleyball in the schools and communities in Mangaung area with the purpose of exposing them to the district, province and national for selection in the teams. Gangsterism, alcohol and drug abuse has escalated in Mangaung, and the club's main issue is to save this youth from this pandemonium. Some of the players are adopted by the owner of the club as they are orphans. The objective of the Club is to facilitate, for the benefit of its members, long-term, sustainable, aquatic-related activity for family recreation and competitive sport, providing members with associated facilities, whilst preserving the natural environment, and the individual rights of people as embodied in the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa.
